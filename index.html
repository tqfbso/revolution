The enemy base is off the map until one of your units sees it: scout. The AI menu (top right) sets the opponent and starts a new game.
Economy: select trucks, right click a stockpile (tan marker) or an oil well (amber) to haul; the buttons at the bottom build. Click one of your buildings to produce units.
Supplies run out, wells pump slowly (hold more wells), towns pay credits for technology.
You command red. Drag: select · Double click: all of that type · Shift: add · Ctrl+A: all
Right click: move (breaks off a fight) · Right click an enemy: attack it · C then click or Alt+right click: attack-move · X: stop · H: hold position · G: hold fire · . (full stop): idle trucks · Esc: clear
Right click a building: infantry moves in · Ctrl+right click a building or bridge: shell it
Ctrl+right click the ground: guns shell that spot (X to cease fire) · F: guns lay smoke at the pointer, which blinds both sides
Field works: select trucks or engineers (Barracks), pick a work at the bottom and drag a line on the ground. Walls and gates stop everything (gates open for you), barbed wire stops men, tank obstacles stop vehicles, trenches and sandbags are cover: right click them with infantry to man them
Right click an enemy work: tanks and guns shoot it down, engineers blow it up. Tanks drive over wire. Units ordered past a wall with no way round breach it themselves if they can
Right click a carrier (or L with squads and carriers selected): mount up · U: dismount
Drones fly over everything; a red ring on the ground is an enemy jammer: drones die inside it
Ctrl or Alt + 1..9: save group · 1..9: recall · twice: jump to it · Minimap: click to look, right click to order
Space: pause. Orders given while paused all start when you resume, so you can plan a line of works or an attack in peace. Speed 0.75x slows the whole game a little
WASD / arrows / screen edge: pan · Wheel: zoom · Q/E or middle drag: rotate
