How to playF1 or the ? button to close

      

        Drag select
        Double click all of that type
        Right click move, or attack what is under it
        C / Alt+right click: attack-move
        Shift+right click: way point
        Ctrl+right click: shell it
        X stop · H hold · G hold fire
        , movement (convoy, wedge, line...)
        N new commander · M board
        P production · K grid
        Option+0..9 save group · 0..9 recall
        Space pause · Backspace go to alert
        Shift+Backspace event log
        WASD pan · wheel zoom · Q/E turn
        Y see through buildings
      

      

        Rest the pointer on a unit or a building to read what it is. Rings on the ground: amber what
        the selected guns and missiles reach (broken red: too close for them), blue what scouts see,
        orange an ambush you laid. The ring an order leaves: green move, amber attack-move, red
        attack or shell, blue into a house or carrier.
      

      

        Fighting in towns
        

          Right click a building: infantry moves in. Right click one the enemy holds: they storm it,
          room by room; bring three men for every defender, fewer if a tank has breached a wall or
          smoke covers the way in. Engineers count as four riflemen.
        

        

          Buildings come apart slowly: shot up, gutted, then a ruin, and each still shelters men.
          Shells and rockets take a building from the top down. Fire from close by (a tank with its
          muzzle on the wall, an engineer's charge, a vehicle blowing up beside it) eats the
          ground floor: below half it catches fire, the fire climbs a floor every six
          seconds and drives the garrison out unhurt, and nobody goes back in for forty seconds.
          When the ground floor is gone the whole building pancakes, whatever health it had
          left, and kills those inside. Mending shores it up.
        

        

          Shift+R then right click a building: squads go up on the roof (further reach and sight,
          rockets hit a tank's top, a mortar can fire; but no walls round them). Shift+H then drag a
          circle: ambush, fire held until an enemy vehicle enters it. B then right click: repair
          (trucks, engineers), then engineers fortify. O: engineers demolish a building, a bridge, a
          wood or a wall; people still in a house brought down are killed, and you pay for them.
          Right click street rubble with engineers to clear it.
        

      

      

        Fire support and the air
        

          Ctrl+right click the ground (or an enemy) with guns or mortars: they shell it, seen or
          not. X ceases fire. F: smoke at the pointer, which blinds both sides. I: a flare over the
          pointer for 30 seconds (it lights your troops too).
        

        

          Drones fly over everything; a red ring on the ground is an enemy jammer and drones die in
          it. Helicopters (tier 2, from a Helipad, three a pad): the attack helicopter hunts
          vehicles and men in the open, the transport lifts four squads (L board, U set down).
          Paratroopers (Barracks, tier 2): J then click the map. Air strike (tier 3, HQ standing):
          Shift+J then click; the alliance drops one guided bomb, Russia a stick of six, China a
          swarm of eight drones. Air defence under the path shoots the plane down first.
        

      

      

        Commanders
        

          Select troops and press N: they get a commander (eight at most). The board top right has a
          card for each; with units selected each card shows a plus that puts them under it. Click a
          card, pick a mission, click the map. Attack forms up, marches as one body, assaults with
          smoke and guns, holds what it took and breaks off when losing. Move (T) goes without
          stopping to fight. Concentrate (R) sends everything to one spot at once. Hold stays to the
          last; Defend gives ground to one far too strong and comes back; Patrol goes back and
          forth; Screen is a thin line of lookouts. Replace losses orders what it loses again. An
          order you give a unit yourself takes it from its commander.
        

      

      

        Economy, supply and production
        

          Select trucks, right click a stockpile (tan) or an oil well (amber) to haul; the buttons
          at the bottom build. Shift while placing puts down several, built in order. Click one of
          your buildings to produce, or P for every factory on one screen: order ahead of your money
          (each unit is paid as it starts), Keep holds that many in the field, Send to routes new
          units to a town or a commander. Towns pay credits for technology.
        

        

          The top bar shows Net: supplies a minute coming in (trucks and flags) less upkeep.
          Troops are in supply within 150 m of a depot or HQ, a little past a town you hold, and
          within 50 m of a truck that is itself within 260 m of one of those. Beyond it they have
          two minutes of rations, then fire at half rate and vehicles crawl. In supply and out of
          the fight, troops mend. Every army point above 60 costs upkeep.
        

        

          Field works: trucks or engineers, pick a work and drag a line. Walls and gates stop
          everything, wire stops men, tank obstacles stop vehicles, trenches and sandbags are cover
          (right click them with infantry). V with engineers: a hidden surveillance camera.
        

      

      

        Armies, night and weather
        

          The Western alliance: fewer, better, dearer units, the toughest tank, long sight, only a
          light jammer. Russia: cheap fast-firing tanks, rocket batteries, the strongest jammer, no
          transport helicopter. China: the cheapest infantry, cheap swarm drones, fast wheeled
          carriers, no paratroopers. The rebels: quick cheap fighters, at home in towns, roadside
          bombs, no air attack.
        

        

          At night troops see six tenths as far; whatever is lit (a unit that just fired, a burning
          wreck, a flare) is seen at daytime range. Rain cuts sight to 85%, snow 80%, fog 55%.
        

      

      

        Setting up, co-op and versus
        

          The enemy base is hidden until one of your units sees it: scout. Main menu, Skirmish: map,
          armies, time, weather, opponent and a layout number (the same number gives the same
          ground). Three or four armies is a free-for-all on the four corners map; you win when the
          last enemy HQ falls.
        

        

          Co-op with friends gives one link for everyone; you command one army together against the
          computer, each with a role. A box takes your own units and leaves your teammates' (theirs
          carry a ring in their colour). Give and Take hand units over. Z marks a point for
          everyone; 1, 2 or 3 just after makes it Attack, Defend or Help me. Versus: red against
          blue, no computer, each side sees only what its troops see. Pause and speed are shared.
        

        
F3 (or a click on the frame-rate line): the full performance readout.
