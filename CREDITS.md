# Credits

## Map data

The Kowloon map (`?map=kowloon`) is built from OpenStreetMap data: its coastline, streets,
park, pools and the footprints and heights of its buildings.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence
(https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The derived tables are in
`src/sim/kowloon-data.ts`, made by `tools/kowloon/osm2map.py`.

## Everything else

Models, textures, sounds and code are the project's own, made in code, or CC0.
